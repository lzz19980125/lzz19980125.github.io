Here is a categorized list of courses and resources for learning about deep learning and related fields.

### Foundations (Optimization & Math)

- [The Iris Book (Matrix Power)](https://d2l.ai/) *(Note: This is likely a reference to a foundational text, possibly "Dive into Deep Learning" or another book focusing on the underlying mathematics like matrix calculus.)*
- [CMU 10-725: Convex Optimization](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~ryantibs/convexopt/)
- [Stanford CS109: Probability for Computer Scientists](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs109/cs109.1226/)
- [UCB EECS 126: Probability and Random Processes](https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~ee126/fa20/content.html)

### Deep Learning (General & Introductory)

- [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/) *(Note: This is the official English name for "动手学习深度学习")*
- [MIT 6.S191: Intro to Deep Learning](http://introtodeeplearning.com/)
- [Stanford CS229: Machine Learning](https://cs229.stanford.edu/)
- [Stanford CS 230: Deep Learning](https://cs230.stanford.edu/)
- [UCB CS 182: Deep Neural Networks](https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs182/sp23/)

### Deep Learning (Advanced Topics)

- [MIT 6.S898/6.7960: Deep Learning](https://phillipi.github.io/6.s898/)
- [Stanford CS 330: Deep Multi-Task and Meta Learning](https://cs330.stanford.edu/)
- [Stanford CS329D: ML Under Distribution Shifts](https://thashim.github.io/cs329D/)

### Reinforcement Learning

- [MIT 6.7920: Reinforcement Learning: Foundations And Methods (Fall 2024)](https://web.mit.edu/6.7920/www/schedule.html)
- [Hands-on Reinforcement Learning](https://github.com/boyu-ai/Hands-on-RL) *(Note: Assumed to be a reference to a hands-on course/book like this one)*
- [UCB CS 285: Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse-fa21/)
- [UCB Deep RL BootCamp](https://sites.google.com/view/deep-rl-bootcamp/lectures)
- [OpenAI Spinning up Reinforcement Learning](https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/)
- [U Toronto STA 4273: Minimizing Expectations (Reinforcement Learning) (Winter 2021)](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~cmaddis/courses/sta4273_w21/)

### Natural Language Processing **& Large Language Models**

- [Stanford CS336 | Language Modeling from Scratch](https://stanford-cs336.github.io/spring2025/)
- [CMU 11-667: Large Language Models: Methods and Applications](https://cmu-llms.org/schedule/)
- [MIT 6.S986: Large Language Models and Beyond](https://ai4comm.media.mit.edu/index.html#course)
- [Stanford CS 324: Large language models](https://stanford-cs324.github.io/winter2022/) 
- [Stanford CS224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/index.html#schedule)
- [Stanford CS25: Transformers United V2](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs25/prev_years/2023_winter/index.html)

### Computer Vision

- [MIT 6.8300/6.8301: Advances in Computer Vision](https://advances-in-vision.github.io/)
- [Stanford CS 231N: Deep Learning for Computer Vision](http://cs231n.stanford.edu/)

### Generative Models

- [MIT 6.S978: Deep Generative Models](https://mit-6s978.github.io/schedule.html)
- [Stanford CS 236G: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)](https://cs236g.stanford.edu/)
- [Stanford CS 236: Deep Generative Models](https://deepgenerativemodels.github.io/)
- [Columbia STAT 8201: Deep Generative Models (GAN)](http://stat.columbia.edu/~cunningham/teaching/GR8201/)

### ML Systems, Data Engineering & Hardware

- [MIT 6.5930/1: Hardware Architecture for Deep Learning](https://csg.csail.mit.edu/6.5930/readinglist.html)
- [MIT 6.S079: Software Systems for Data Science](https://dsg.csail.mit.edu/6.S079/notes.php)
- [MIT 6.S984: Datacenter Computing](https://sites.google.com/view/datacenter-computing-spring-23/home)
- [CMU 10-714: Deep Learning Systems](https://dlsyscourse.org/lectures/)
- [Stanford CS246: Mining Massive Data Sets (by Jure Leskovec)](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs246/)
- [Stanford CS 329S: Machine Learning Systems Design](https://stanford-cs329s.github.io/syllabus.html)

### Edge Computing & Efficient ML

- [MIT 6.S965: TinyML and Efficient Deep Learning Computing](https://efficientml.ai/)

### Graph Neural Networks & Graph ML

- [Stanford CS224W: Machine Learning with Graphs (by Jure Leskovec)](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224w/)

### Data Science & Visualization

- [MIT 6.859: Interactive Data Visualization](http://vis.csail.mit.edu/classes/6.859/)
- [Stanford CS 448B: Visualization](https://magrawala.github.io/cs448b-fa21/)

### Robotics & Embodied AI

- [MIT 6.S953: Embodied Intelligence](https://phillipi.github.io/6.s953/2024/schedule.html)

### AI Ethics & Society

- [Stanford: Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (Fairness & Differential privacy & Federated Learning)](https://stanfordaiethics.github.io/syllabus.html)

### Computer Science Fundamentals

- [Stanford CS45: Software Tools Every Programmer Should Know](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/)
